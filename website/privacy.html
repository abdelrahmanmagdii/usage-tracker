Privacy policy

UsageBar is a Mac menu bar app by Abdelrahman Amer. It does not create an account with us and does not send your quota, credentials, or file paths to a UsageBar server — there isn't one.

What the app reads on your Mac

To show meters, UsageBar talks to tools you already installed:

  • Codex CLI over its local app server.
  • Claude Code credentials from macOS Keychain or ~/.claude, then Anthropic's usage endpoint.
  • Cursor's local login database, then Cursor's usage API.
  • OpenCode Go's local auth.json, then OpenCode's usage API.

Those third parties process usage according to their own policies. UsageBar does not get a copy.

What is stored locally

Preferences, a short quota history used to spot surprise resets, and a Tibo Watch cache live in this app's container / WebView storage on your Mac.

Network

The app fetches provider usage endpoints and a public JSON feed of reset announcements from GitHub. Share actions open X or LinkedIn in your browser; we do not upload the snapshot. You paste it.

Children

UsageBar is a developer utility. It is not directed at children under 13.
